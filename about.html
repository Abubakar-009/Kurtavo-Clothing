Our brand story

Kurtavo Clothing started as a small shop in Rawalpindi with one idea: everyday kurtas and casual wear should look sharp, fit well and last.

We choose local fabrics and work with a small team of five, so every piece is checked by hand before it reaches you in the shop or at your door.

Premium quality

Ethical sourcing

Fast delivery
